About

We didn't study law firms.
We were built inside one.

Most legal AI is built in a lab, then sold to lawyers. Legwork happened the other way around. For twenty-two years, our first design partner ran its entire practice, every docket entry, every filing, every deadline, every fee, through systems that quietly accumulated 3.5 TB of structured legal memory.

We turned that memory into a working AI, first for that firm, now for yours. Not a chat window beside the work. An agent inside it, one that has seen how an examiner responds, how a deadline lands, how a fee gets realised, thousands of times over.

That's why we're called Legwork. The judgment stays with the lawyer. Everything else, the searching, the drafting, the tracking, the chasing, is legwork. And we do the legwork.
