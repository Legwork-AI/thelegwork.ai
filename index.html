FAQ

Questions firms ask.

What is Legwork?

Legwork is an AI platform built for Indian legal practice. Legwork's deepest capability today is in trademarks, patents, and trademark litigation, covering docketing, prosecution strategy, drafting, deadline tracking, and case status.

Why is Legwork focused on IP practice?

Because depth beats breadth in legal AI. IP is process-heavy, deadline-dense, and unforgiving of shallow tools, which makes it the hardest and most valuable place to get right. We chose to be the best in the world at IP before being average at everything.

Can Legwork connect to the systems our firm already uses?

Yes. Legwork is built to integrate with your firm's existing accounting, case management, HR, and CRM systems, or we'll build the connection for you where one doesn't already exist. No rip-and-replace.

Does Legwork replace our existing systems, like our current docketing or accounting software?

No. Legwork is built to run alongside the systems your firm already uses. It works as an overlay on top of your existing tools rather than requiring you to rip out and replace them.

Is our client data used to train shared AI models?

No. Client data stays inside your firm's own environment and is never used to train models shared across other firms or customers.

How is client data protected?

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, with role-based access controls and a full audit trail on every action. Data residency is in India, and confidentiality and privilege are treated as the default, not an optional setting.

Does Legwork handle courtroom or litigation work?

We're currently focused on trademark litigation support. If trial court or Supreme Court practice is relevant to your firm, reach out and we'll walk you through where that work stands today.

We work across jurisdictions. Does Legwork handle work outside India?

Legwork is built in India and is deepest in Indian IP practice today. IP law is broadly the same across jurisdictions; what changes from country to country is mostly procedure. That is exactly the layer Legwork is built to learn. If your practice spans jurisdictions, reach out and we'll walk you through what Legwork carries for you today.

We're a firm outside India. Can we use Legwork?

Yes, if Indian IP is part of your work. Firms abroad use Indian counsel for filings, prosecution, and enforcement in India, and Legwork carries that Indian side end to end. If you'd like Legwork for your own home jurisdiction, reach out and tell us where you practise.
